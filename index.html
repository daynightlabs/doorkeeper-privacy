Doorkeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Doorkeeper App Lock, published by Daynight Labs ("the app"), locks the apps you choose on your Android phone behind a PIN or fingerprint.

Data we collect

None. Doorkeeper does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and contains no ads, analytics or third-party code.

Data stored on your device

  • Your PIN and recovery code, stored only as salted, slow hashes (never in plain text).
  • The list of apps you chose to lock.
  • Your settings (lock on/off, pause time, automatic locking of new apps, protection of Settings and the installer, fingerprint unlock for apps and for Doorkeeper itself, and that you accepted the in-app disclosure).
  • A short list (last 50) of wrong PIN entries: the time and which app was being opened.
  • A count of recent wrong PIN entries, used to slow down repeated guessing.
  • A count of how many times the phone blocked Doorkeeper's PIN screen, used only to show you the fix for your phone.

This information never leaves your device. It is excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Fingerprint

If you turn on fingerprint unlock, Doorkeeper uses Android's own fingerprint prompt. Doorkeeper never sees, stores or receives fingerprint data; Android only tells it whether the fingerprint matched.

Accessibility permission

Doorkeeper uses Android's Accessibility service only to detect the name of the app that comes to the screen. If it is an app you locked, Doorkeeper shows its PIN screen. Doorkeeper does not read screen content, keystrokes, passwords, messages, contacts or any other personal data through this permission. Nothing it detects ever leaves your phone; the only thing it keeps is the short list of wrong PIN entries described above, which you can see and clear in the app. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

Children

Doorkeeper is a general-purpose tool for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.
